Corporate Gifting

Corporate Gifting, Delivered Anywhere in Pakistan

One vendor for Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and every city in between. 600+ brands trust us with their gifting.

Coordinating corporate gifts for a team spread across multiple cities is usually the hard part, not picking the gift itself. Reverb Merch handles sourcing, branding, packaging, and delivery from a single production base in Karachi, so a client in Lahore and a client in Islamabad can get the same quality, the same branding, and the same delivery date without you managing three different vendors.

Where We Deliver

We ship corporate gift orders nationwide, including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, and Multan. Whether it's a single executive gift box couriered to a client's office or a bulk order split across regional branches, delivery is coordinated centrally so nothing falls through the cracks between cities.

What We Gift

  • Executive gift sets — premium boxed sets with flasks, notebooks, and pens for clients and leadership.
  • Festive and seasonal gifting — Eid, New Year, and anniversary gift boxes with custom branding.
  • Tech gifting — power banks, wireless chargers, and branded accessories for a modern gift.
  • Bulk client gifting — consistent, on-brand gift sets for large distributor or partner networks.

Why Companies Choose Reverb Merch for Nationwide Gifting

Every order goes through the same process regardless of where it's shipping: you share a brief, we design a mockup with your logo within 24 hours, you approve it, and we produce and dispatch. That consistency is what makes multi-city gifting manageable, one point of contact, one quality standard, one invoice.
