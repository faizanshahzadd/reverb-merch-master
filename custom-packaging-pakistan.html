Custom Packaging

The Box Is Part of the Gift

Branded rigid boxes, tissue paper, and filler for a premium unboxing moment.

The same gift feels completely different depending on what it arrives in. A branded rigid box with tissue paper and a ribbon reads as premium before the recipient even sees what's inside, a plain plastic bag doesn't. We design and produce custom packaging for companies across Pakistan, either as a standalone packaging order or bundled with a full gift set.

What We Produce

  • Rigid gift boxes — custom-branded, magnetic-close or lift-off lid options.
  • Tissue paper — branded or color-matched to your palette.
  • Ribbon and seals — for the final finishing touch.
  • Custom inserts — foam or cardboard inserts cut to hold specific items securely in transit.

Packaging That Survives Delivery

A beautiful box that arrives crushed defeats the purpose. We factor in courier handling when designing inserts and outer packaging, especially for gifts shipping outside Karachi, so what the recipient unboxes looks exactly like what left our workshop.

Real Examples

See packaging examples including the EXD gift box and Macpac pen box in our portfolio. Most clients pair custom packaging with a corporate gift set or onboarding kit, but standalone packaging-only orders are common for companies who already have the product and just need premium presentation.
