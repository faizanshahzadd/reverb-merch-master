About Us

Built for Brands That Care About Impressions

Karachi-based, Pakistan-focused, and obsessed with the details other vendors skip.

Reverb Merch is a corporate gifting and branded merchandise company based in Karachi, serving clients across Pakistan. We handle the full process: sourcing, branding, packaging, and delivery, so you brief us once and we handle the rest.

What We Believe

Branded merchandise shouldn't be an afterthought. When you hand someone a gift, whether it's a client, a new hire, or an exhibition visitor, it should make them remember your brand for the right reasons: quality, thoughtfulness, and attention to detail.

Who We Work With

Over 4+ years, we've delivered merchandise for 600+ clients across Pakistan, from tech startups and BPOs to universities and manufacturing brands, ranging from 10-unit luxury kits to 500-unit onboarding programmes.

How We Work

Every order goes through the same process: you share a brief, we design mockups with your logo within 24 hours, you review and approve, and we produce, pack, and deliver on time. No surprises, no vague timelines.
