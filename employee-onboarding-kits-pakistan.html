Employee Kits

Onboarding Kits New Hires Actually Keep

Welcome boxes for companies across Pakistan, from 10-unit leadership kits to 500-unit rollouts.

The first thing a new hire unboxes sets the tone for how they see your company. A generic pen and a photocopied handbook says one thing; a well-branded welcome kit with a notebook, drinkware, and a personal touch says another. We design and produce employee onboarding kits for companies across Pakistan, from single-city startups to teams spread across Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad.

What Goes Into a Good Onboarding Kit

  • Branded notebook or planner — practical, used daily, keeps your logo visible.
  • Drinkware — insulated flasks or mugs are consistently the most-used item in any kit.
  • Apparel — a branded t-shirt, cap, or polo for day-one team photos.
  • Tech accessory — a power bank or USB drive adds real perceived value.
  • Personal touch — a printed welcome card or handwritten note from the team.

Built for Scale

Whether you're onboarding two people a month or running a 500-unit rollout for a new office, the process is the same: share your brief, approve a mockup with your logo within 24 hours, and we handle production and delivery. See real onboarding kits we've delivered, including for Mozack, Fiber Mountain, and Ibex, in our portfolio.

Minimum Order and Timelines

Onboarding kits start at 10 units for premium sets and 50 units for standard single-item orders, with standard production taking 10-14 working days. Read our full breakdown of what to include in an onboarding kit on our blog, or check the FAQ page for more on timelines and file requirements.
