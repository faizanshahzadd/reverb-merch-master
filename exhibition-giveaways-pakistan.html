Exhibition Merch

Giveaways People Keep, Not Bin at the Door

Booth merch and event swag for exhibitions and trade shows across Pakistan.

Most exhibition giveaways are forgettable because they're picked for cost, not for whether anyone will actually use them. We work with exhibitors across Pakistan to produce giveaways people hold onto, which keeps your brand in front of them long after the event ends.

What Works at a Booth

  • Tech accessories — power banks and USB drives are consistently the highest-retention giveaway category.
  • Drinkware — branded flasks and tumblers get daily use long after the show.
  • Tote bags — practical, visible, and doubles as free advertising as attendees carry it around the venue.
  • Premium keychains and pins — low-cost, high-retention options for high-traffic booths.

Built Around Event Deadlines

Exhibition orders are rarely flexible on timing, the event date doesn't move. We prioritize exhibition and event orders and recommend reaching out as early as possible once your booth is confirmed so we can lock in production slots ahead of the date. See a real GCC exhibition kit we produced for Unifonic in our portfolio.

Matching Your Booth Branding

Giveaways work best when they match the rest of your booth, same colors, same logo placement, same overall feel. We design mockups with your exact branding before production starts, so what's on the table matches what's on the banner.
