Convocation Merch

Graduation Merch Your Batch Will Actually Wear

Graduation shirts, batch jackets, and convocation gift sets for universities across Pakistan.

Convocation only happens once, and the merch from it either gets worn for years or shoved in a drawer. We've produced graduation shirts, batch jackets, and convocation gift sets for university societies and student groups across Pakistan, working from a single design brief for an entire batch, which usually means dozens or hundreds of matching units in one run.

What We Produce for Convocations

  • Graduation and batch shirts — custom design per batch, department, or society.
  • Batch jackets — a common convocation keepsake request, embroidered with batch name and year.
  • Convocation gift sets — small keepsakes or accessories for graduating students.
  • Society and event merch — for student societies running their own branded merchandise.

Working With Student Organizers

Convocation orders are usually coordinated by a student committee working within a fixed budget and a fixed date, sizes need to be collected in advance, and the whole batch needs to be ready before the ceremony. We keep the process simple: one point of contact, a mockup approved before production, and delivery scheduled around your convocation date, not after it.
