University & Convocation Merch: A Guide for Student Societies

From graduation stoles to society welcome kits — sourcing merch for a campus, not a company.

← Back to Blog

Universities are one of the most underrated buyers of branded merchandise — convocations, orientation weeks, society drives, and alumni events all need merch, but the sourcing process usually falls to a student society treasurer or an admin office with a tight budget and an even tighter deadline. Here's how to do it without the usual last-minute scramble.

1. Convocation Merch Has a Fixed, Unmovable Date

Unlike a corporate order that can slip a week if needed, a convocation date is set months in advance and does not move. Stoles, medals, photo frames, and gift boxes for graduating batches need to be ordered with real buffer time — production delays that would be a minor inconvenience for a corporate client become a real problem when the ceremony has a fixed date and hundreds of guests.

2. Society Merch Needs to Survive Multiple Cohorts

A student society's branding often needs to work across several intakes — this year's hoodie design should still look reasonable to next year's members. Simple, durable designs (screen-printed logos over complex full-prints) tend to age better and cost less to reorder each semester.

3. Budgets Are Real, and So Is Bulk

Student societies and admin offices are usually working with fixed, modest budgets — but often need larger quantities than a typical corporate order (a batch jacket run for an entire graduating class, for instance). Getting a transparent per-unit price upfront, rather than negotiating from scratch each time, makes budgeting to a society's actual funds much easier.

4. Welcome Kits Set the Tone for Freshers

Orientation week kits — a notebook, lanyard, and a small welcome card — give new students something tangible on day one. It doesn't need to be elaborate; it needs to be organized enough that 200+ kits can be handed out in a single morning without confusion.

5. One Vendor Relationship Beats Reordering From Scratch

Universities and societies that come back every semester save real time by working with one merchandise partner who already has their logo files, past specs, and delivery preferences on record — instead of re-explaining the brief to a new vendor every convocation season.
