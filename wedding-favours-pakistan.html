Wedding Favours

Wedding Favours Guests Remember, Not Regift

Personalised gift boxes and engraved keepsakes for baraat, walima, and mehndi.

Wedding favours are easy to get wrong, either too generic to remember or too expensive to justify at scale for a large guest list. We design personalised wedding favour kits for weddings across Pakistan, from a manageable close-family batch to full baraat and walima guest counts.

Popular Wedding Favour Options

  • Engraved keepsakes — small personalised items with the couple's names and wedding date.
  • Custom gift boxes — dry fruits, chocolates, or small mementos in a branded box.
  • Personalised packaging — matching the wedding's color theme and typography.
  • Mehndi and baraat specific favours — smaller, lighter gifts suited to each event.

Designed Around Your Theme

Every wedding has its own colour palette and typography, from the invitation cards to the stage decor. We match favour packaging to that theme rather than offering one generic design across every order, so the favours feel like part of the event, not an afterthought handed out at the door.

Planning at Scale

Guest counts for Pakistani weddings can run into the hundreds, which means favour orders need to be planned well ahead of the event date, not the week before. We recommend reaching out at least three to four weeks before your function to leave enough time for design approval and production. For pricing on smaller custom orders, see our pricing page, or reach out directly for a quote based on your guest count.
