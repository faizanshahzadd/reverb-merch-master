FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Our minimum order is 10 units for premium kits and 50 units for standard single-item orders. We also accept custom quotes for larger volume orders.

How long does production take?

Standard orders take 10–14 working days. Rush orders (7 days) available for certain products. Exhibition and event orders: please contact us as early as possible.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes, we deliver across Pakistan, with Karachi-based production and nationwide logistics coordination.

What file format do you need for the logo?

We prefer vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) for the cleanest output. PNG files with transparent backgrounds also work well.

Can I see a sample before the full order?

Yes. We produce a physical sample for most products before full production. Sample costs are credited towards your order if you proceed.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, Easypaisa, and JazzCash. 50% advance on confirmation, balance before dispatch.
