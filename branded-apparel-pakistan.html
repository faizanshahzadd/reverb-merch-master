Branded Apparel

Team Wear That Looks Good On and Off the Clock

T-shirts, polos, hoodies, and caps, embroidered or printed, for teams across Pakistan.

Uniform team apparel does two jobs at once: it makes your staff look consistent in front of clients, and it turns every employee into a walking, moving piece of brand visibility. We produce branded apparel for companies across Pakistan, from a single event t-shirt run to full uniform rollouts for growing teams.

What We Produce

  • Polo shirts — the most common uniform request, available in embroidered or screen-printed logo finishes.
  • T-shirts — for events, campaigns, and casual team wear.
  • Hoodies and jackets — for winter uniform sets or premium team gifting.
  • Caps — embroidered branding for field teams, riders, or promotional staff.

Embroidery vs. Printing

Embroidery holds up better over repeated washes and looks more premium on polos and caps, screen printing and DTG (direct-to-garment) are more cost-effective for larger runs or multi-color designs. We'll recommend the right finish based on your logo, budget, and how the apparel will actually be used, whether that's daily office wear or a one-off event.

Real Examples

We've produced branded apparel for teams including Noon Academy, see the full kit in our portfolio. Most apparel orders are bundled into a broader onboarding kit alongside notebooks and drinkware, but we also take standalone apparel-only orders.
